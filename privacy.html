Helm Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Helm is an iPad app for controlling your own computers. Helm does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What stays on your iPad

  • The computers you add (names, addresses, user names, settings) are saved on your iPad.
  • Passwords and Helm's SSH key are saved in the iPad's Keychain.
  • Preview pictures of your computers' screens are saved on your iPad and never leave it.

Deleting Helm removes this data.

Network connections

Helm connects only to the computers you choose, directly from your iPad. Nothing passes through servers run by the developer, and there are none. Helm has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Helm AI

Helm AI runs on your iPad with Apple Intelligence (Apple's on-device model). Your questions, the commands it runs and what they print stay on your iPad and your own Mac; nothing is sent to the developer or to an AI service.

Permissions

  • Local Network: to find computers on your network and connect to them.
  • Camera: only when you scan a QR code to add a computer. Pictures are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: only if you turn on the app lock. Helm never sees your biometric data.
  • Helm passcode: if you set one, only a scrambled form of it is kept, in the Keychain of your iPad. It never leaves the device.

Children

Helm is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact
